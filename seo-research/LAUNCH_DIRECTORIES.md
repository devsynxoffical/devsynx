# Launch & Directory Kit (the "50 sites" list)

Most sites in that list are **launch and review sites for software products and AI tools**
(G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, AI tool directories). They reject a plain "agency" listing,
but DEVSYNX has two real free tools they *will* accept:

- App Development Cost Calculator: https://devsynx.com/tools/app-development-cost-calculator/
- Website Cost Calculator: https://devsynx.com/tools/website-cost-calculator/

So: **list the company** where companies are allowed (Tier 1), and **launch the free tools**
where products are required (Tier 2/3). Each listing links back to devsynx.com.

Rules: same name, phone and address everywhere (see LISTINGS.md). Use info@devsynx.com.
Do 3–5 sites a day, not 50 in one day. Log each live URL in the table at the bottom.

## Tier 3 — AI directories (only once an AI product/demo page exists)

Dang.ai, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify, Toolpilot and similar accept **AI tools only**.
Do not submit the agency there — they reject it. When we publish a public AI voice-agent demo page,
submit that page.

## Skip / not applicable

Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice and TrustRadius list **software you sell to users**, not service
companies. Revisit only if DEVSYNX launches its own SaaS product.
