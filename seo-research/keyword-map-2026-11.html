How to make city and country pages rank

  1. Unique content per page. Write 800+ words that are actually about that city: local clients, case studies, industries there, prices in the local currency, a local FAQ. Swapping only the city name in a template gets the pages filtered as doorway pages.
  2. Google Business Profile. City keywords in Pakistan show a map pack. Islamabad is your verified location. For Lahore and Karachi you need a real address (a co-working desk works) to appear on the map; without one, the organic page can still rank.
  3. Schema. Add LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService with an areaServed per page, plus FAQPage and Service.
  4. Internal links. Home → /global and Pakistan hubs → city pages → service pages. Every city page links to the 3–5 most relevant case studies.
  5. Local backlinks. Pakistan: PSEB and P@SHA listings, Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, local directories (Rozee, OLX Business). UAE/US/UK: Clutch reviews from clients in those countries are the strongest single signal.
  6. Separate app and web. Overseas, "app development company" and "web development company" are different buyers. Keep separate pages per country rather than one combined "services" page.
  7. hreflang. If you add en-AE, en-GB and en-US variants, declare hreflang alternates so the pages don't compete with each other.

Method and limits

  • Volumes are Google Ads monthly averages for the whole country. A city keyword counts everyone in that country who typed the city name, so people who search "software house" from inside Lahore without the city name aren't counted.
  • Measured: Pakistan (306 keywords tested, 159 with data), US (107 / 98), UK (107 / 80), UAE (99 / 67), Canada (99 / 67).
  • Not measured: Saudi Arabia (the data source only accepts Arabic keywords there), Australia and Germany (OpenSEO credits ran out). Both connected OpenSEO accounts now have about 4 credits left.
  • Current rankings for devsynx.com weren't checked for the same reason. Connect Google Search Console in OpenSEO to see which of these keywords already get impressions.
